Numerical Toys

Small interactive demos for numerical methods and computational mathematics.

  • Finite Difference Coefficient Generator
    Compute finite difference coefficients for arbitrary stencils.
  • Euler Riemann Solver (Exact)
    Interactive visualization of the exact solution of the Euler Riemann problem.
  • Three-Body Simulation
    Numerical simulation of gravitational three-body dynamics.
  • ColorManager Palette Browser
    Browse a large dataset of curated color palettes with interactive swatches and curves.
